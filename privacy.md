# TimmyBread Uploader - Privacy Policy

TimmyBread Uploader collects only the data needed to upload videos you choose: your basic TikTok profile info (open id, display name) and an access token issued by TikTok.

Tokens are stored privately on the owner's machine and are used only to post the owner's own videos. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings, and you can ask for deletion of stored tokens at 2aktzq9z@mail.grokbot.com.
